## Suggested interpretation

These cutoffs should be described as role-specific, distribution-based relative high-performance definitions. The table shows the empirical WAR and utilization profiles induced by the current thresholds. It does not establish that 40% or 20% is a universal or statistically optimal MLB success threshold.
